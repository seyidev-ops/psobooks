A word from the author Abuja, Nigeria

Beloved, welcome.

For more than thirty years I have stood in the pulpit of Family Worship Centre and said the same few things in many ways. God starts small. God talks to His friends. The road that leads to life is narrow. And you cannot know Jesus by somebody else's knowing.

These four books are those messages. They were preached on ordinary Sunday mornings in 2004, 2011, 2018 and this year, and now they are set down so you can carry them with you. One of them I preached eleven months after Pastor Ina went to be with the Lord, when I had decided to live for only one reason: to please God.

So read them slowly. Underline. Mark the margins. Say the confessions out loud. You may not be responsible for where you are right now, but you are responsible for how long you stay there. My prayer is that you will not close the last page the same way you opened the first, because you can't relate with Jesus and be the same.

Pastor Sarah Senior Pastor, Family Worship Centre, Abuja
President, Family Ministries International

About the author

Pastor Sarah Omakwu

Pastor Sarah Omakwu is a preacher, conference speaker and spiritual leader who shares the message of God through her own testimony with a new generation of believers. A native of Kaduna, she is the widow of Pastor Inalegwu (Ina) Omakwu of blessed memory.

On 11 April 1993 she and her late husband founded Family Worship Centre in Wuye District, Abuja, today one of the city's liveliest congregations, with branches all over the country. She is the Senior Pastor of Family Worship Centre and President of Family Ministries International.

Her conviction that the gospel must be good news to the weak shapes the church's care for widows, orphans, prisoners and people displaced by conflict. She also runs the Sarina Project, which takes in street girls for rehabilitation. She is blessed with three grown children, Blessing, Favour and Worthy, and many adopted children.

Are these transcripts of the sermons?

No. Each book is drawn from the original recordings and shaped into chapters for readers. Her voice, her stories and her Nigerian expressions stay; the live-service asides go.

In what order should I read them?

Each book stands alone. Many readers begin with Knowing Jesus For Yourself, because it lays the foundation, and then follow the season they are in.

Which Bible translation do the books quote?

Mostly the New King James Version, with the Amplified Bible and other versions wherever Pastor Sarah read from them in the pulpit.

Can my church order in bulk?

Yes. Choose “Church or group order” in the form and include your city. Bulk orders are welcome for small groups, ministries and gifts.
